I'm **Ricardo Carvalho**, a Senior Dynamics 365 Business Central Technical Lead. I spend my days doing NAV-to-BC cloud migrations, AL development, integrations (CRM, payment services, e-commerce) and Azure DevOps pipelines — and a fair share of untangling the issues that support tickets call "impossible".

## What this blog is

The posts here are the long versions of what I publish on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ricardo-carvalho-bc/): real problems from production work, the actual root cause, and the exact fix — with links to the GitHub issues, Microsoft Learn pages and release notes that back every claim.

No marketing. No "unlock your potential". If something is broken or a workaround is ugly, the post says so.
