Everyone is waiting for the 2026 release wave 2 plan. Microsoft is retiring release plans instead.

Starting September 2026, Release Plans will no longer be published. New Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and Dataverse capabilities move to the AI at Work roadmap. Existing plans stay online for historical reference.

The 2026 wave 1 plan covers April through September 2026, right up to where the new roadmap takes over.

Two things matter here.

First, how you track upcoming work changes. If your upgrade and compatibility planning is built around the twice-a-year release plan ritual, that ritual ends this year.

Second, read the GA dates correctly. A month in the General availability column means any day inside that month. Only features showing a full date have shipped. And the plan itself warns that delivery timelines may change and projected functionality may not be released.

For AL developers, the wave 1 highlights are agent-heavy. An enhanced MCP server for agentic experiences lands April 1. More BC data through MCP server enhancements follows June 12. Multiple Sales Order Agents per company, with smarter item matching, arrive July 8. Dataverse field mapping in BC also lands April 1.

How to prepare: read the wave 1 plan now, flag the April 1 items that hit your environments first, and get familiar with the AI at Work roadmap before September.
