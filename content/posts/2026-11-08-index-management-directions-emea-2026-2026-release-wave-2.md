I have lost entire days to slow BC queries where the answer was a database index I could not see or manage from inside the product.

That gap is closing. Business Central 2026 release wave 1 introduced a new Index Management experience that puts visibility and control of database indexes directly in the hands of administrators and developers.

And 2026 release wave 2 expands those capabilities even further.

Why this matters: index problems used to live where developers could not easily reach them. Diagnosing a slow query often meant asking someone with database access what was actually going on. The Directions EMEA 2026 session went straight at that gap: use these capabilities to diagnose performance issues, optimize queries, and improve scalability in real-world environments.

The session, "Microsoft presents: Index management", ran on October 29 at 13:45 in Paris. The abstract also promised a look at upcoming changes to indexing and what they mean for your solutions. Recordings and slides usually land in the Directions app afterwards, so that is where to look.

How to prepare. My list, not Microsoft's:

If you are already on 2026 wave 1, open the Index Management experience and learn what it shows you today. Wave 2 builds on that foundation.

Wait for the recording if you missed the room, then re-read the Update 29.0 notes with indexing in mind. The abstract kept the new capabilities vague, so the specifics are what you are looking for, not the headline.
