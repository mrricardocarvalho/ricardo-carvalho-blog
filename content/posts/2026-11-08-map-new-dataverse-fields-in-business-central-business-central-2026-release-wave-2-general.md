Everyone assumes new Dataverse fields just show up in Business Central. They don't. Update 29.0 lists a way to map new Dataverse fields without creating or updating an integration extension.

The 2026 release wave 2 release notes list it as a single row: Map new Dataverse fields in Business Central, General availability, product area Adapt faster with Power Platform, Roadmap ID 573365.

If you maintain a BC to Dataverse integration, you already know the routine. The schema on the Dataverse side moves forward. New fields appear. The mapping on the BC side does not care until someone does the work.

GA in Update 29.0 means this is not a preview experiment. It rolls out with the wave.

One caveat. The whatsnew table gives you the headline, not a rollout procedure. The notes point you to the roadmap entry behind the ID. So start there.

Open Roadmap ID 573365 in the AI at Work roadmap and read the actual description.

Then do your own inventory. List every Dataverse table you have coupled to BC. Mark the fields that exist on the Dataverse side but are not mapped today.

That last list is your day-one workload when the update lands.
